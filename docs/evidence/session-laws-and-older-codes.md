and [the `state-code-forms` entry](../divergences.md#state-code-forms): 40 statute citations
drawn at random (seed 118) from the 1,579 that citare finds with the #118 entries of
`data/laws-local.json` and didn't find before, over the CAP statute sample (12,337 opinions)
and citation-bench's stare.pub sample (1,000 opinions). Columns: the source opinion, the text
with the citation's full span in `⟦ ⟧`, and the fields citare gives. The last column is
whether the jurisdiction, code, chapter and section (or article) are what the opinion cites.

CAP's OCR of `ch. 56½` as `ch. 5614`, and the chapter is `5614`. Illinois paragraph numbers keep
CAP's U+2014 where the statute has a hyphen (`par. 702—7`), as citare keeps Unicode hyphens in
every section.
