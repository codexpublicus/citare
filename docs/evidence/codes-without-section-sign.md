and [the `state-code-forms` entry](../divergences.md#state-code-forms): 40 statute citations
drawn at random (seed 115) from the 14,808 that citare finds with the #115 entries of
`data/laws-local.json` and didn't find before, over the CAP statute sample (12,337 opinions)
and citation-bench's stare.pub sample (1,000 opinions). Columns: the source opinion, the text
with the citation's full span in `⟦ ⟧`, and the fields citare gives. The last column is
whether the jurisdiction, code, chapter and section are what the opinion cites.

before the parenthesis (rows 24 and 35, `ORS 423.021 (1) (d)` and `LSA-R.S. 15:422 (6)`) are in
the full span but not in the pin cite field; that is the post-citation pattern every statute
citation goes through, not these entries.
